Background

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from IIT Kanpur in 2019, advised by Nitin Saxena. Before joining CMI, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Aalen University (2019–2022), hosted by Thomas Thierauf.

  • Ph.D., Computer Science — IIT Kanpur, 2019
  • M.Tech., Computer Science — IIT Kanpur, 2014
  • B.E., Information Technology — Bengal Engineering and Science University, Shibpur, 2007–2011

Miscellaneous

I come from Simlapal, a small town in Bankura District in West Bengal.

I am a novice listener of Hindustani classical music (Rajan Parrikar’s archive and Gajananbuwa Joshi’s website). I also enjoy old Indian songs by K. L. Saigal, Pankaj Mullick, K. C. Dey, and Hemanta. Kishore Kumar is my most favorite singer.

I enjoy reading fiction and nonfiction, and occasionally some poems too. I use AI to understand English poems.

I like both tea and coffee, mountains and sea. I like asking questions.

Some of my favorite places: Talda, Simlapal, Sarenga, Puri, Benaras, Kanpur, Ulm, Chennai (Siruseri, Thiruvanmiyur, Mylapore, ECR), Mumbai (Colaba), Kolkata (Park Street, north Kolkata), Munnar, Mysore, Regensburg, Berlin, Warwick, San Diego, Tel Aviv, Delhi, Bengaluru (ITCS), Goa, Deoghar, Bankura, Howrah (Shibpur, Belur Math), and Udaipur.
